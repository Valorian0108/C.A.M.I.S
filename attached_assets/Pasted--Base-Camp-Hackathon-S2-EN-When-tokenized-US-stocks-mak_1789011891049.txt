## I. About This Hackathon

US equities have open and close hours, but tokenized US stocks (rToken) turn the trading window into 7×24. Macro events still happen on weekends while prices keep moving on-chain — this is the new frontier S2 is built to explore.

**Bitget AI Base Camp Hackathon S2** focuses on **AI × US stock trading** (including tokenized US stocks / related contract scenarios), with a preference for real, runnable strategies and tools.

This edition features **three Bitget main tracks** (Alpha Factory / Agentic Trading / AI Trading Desk). **Chapter II** is split into **Participant Prizes** and **Audience Engagement**; how to submit and vote is in **Chapter III**; track-specific requirements are in **Chapter IV**.

### Basic Competition Rules

1. **Submission materials**: The form must include a complete **project description** + **submission materials link** (at minimum an accessible Demo; see **Chapter IV** for track requirements). A GitHub repo or X long-form post cannot replace the project description in the form.
2. **Multi-theme participation**: A team may submit to at most 2 different themes; each submission must be an independent project and must be entered through a **separate form submission** (one project and one set of materials per submission). Awards are calculated per **entry**; different entries from the same team are evaluated separately.
3. **No simple reuse of S1 work**: Submissions that directly port S1 entries, or only rename / make minor edits, are not accepted. If continuing an S1 project direction, you must describe **substantive new additions** in the form; judging evaluates based on the new content.

**Positioning:** The LLM is the primary trading decision-maker, not just an assistant. The Agent must sense the environment, make independent judgments, and autonomously place orders with risk controls.

**Submission requirements:** Runnable Demo + demonstrate a complete event → decision → execution flow (simulated or paper trading acceptable)

**Sub-theme guide (select in form at submission; optional, not mandatory):**

| Sub-Theme                     | Core Question                                                                                                      | Example Approaches                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Event-Driven Agent            | How do news / announcements / macro events drive autonomous Agent trading?                                         | Policy speech → LLM interpretation → rebalance; earnings beat → add position; rate decision → hedge rotation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Market Sentiment Agent        | How does real-time social / forum / X sentiment translate into position signals?                                   | FOMO detection → contrarian hedge; sentiment top detection; reduce before overheating                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Earnings-Driven Trading Agent | How does the Agent autonomously interpret earnings / conference calls and execute?                                 | EPS beat → add; guidance cut → reduce; post-earnings anomaly tracking                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Cross-Asset Execution Agent   | How does the Agent manage rToken and Crypto positions simultaneously?                                              | Hedge Crypto when rToken anomalies; dynamic cross-market allocation after macro shocks                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

### Submission FAQ

<details>

<summary>Q: Is an X post required?</summary>

Yes. Submission must include **at least 1** compliant X post link — content must introduce the product / Agent / strategy you're building (interactive promotional post, dev log, demo showcase, etc.), including `#BitgetHackathon` and `@Bitget_AI`. You must also retweet \[TBD: X link]. Pure retweets or posts with no substantive introduction = incomplete submission.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: How many tracks / themes can I enter?</summary>

A team may submit to at most 2 different themes; each must be an independent project entered through a separate form submission.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: Can I resubmit an S1 project with minor changes?</summary>

Simply renaming or making minor edits to an S1 project **does not count as a valid submission**. If continuing an S1 direction, you must describe **substantive new additions** in the form; judging evaluates based on the new content.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: Must I pick a sub-theme? What award if I don't?</summary>

Not mandatory. Pick a named sub-theme → compete for Theme Prize (1 winner per sub-theme); pick Open Theme → compete for Open Theme Prize (2 open slots per track, top 1 each). Award rules in **Chapter II**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: Where does the project description go?</summary>

Must be written completely in the Google Form "Project Description" field; GitHub README or X Thread cannot substitute.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: How do I submit materials?</summary>

Put all links in the single **"Submission Materials Link"** field (Demo, GitHub, video, backtest, paper trading logs, etc.); required materials by track in **"Track Materials ↔ How to Fill the Form"** in this chapter.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: What fields are in the form?</summary>

Subject to the official Google Form link. Beyond team and project basics, **target user & product value** and **validation data & key metrics** are written as parts 2 and 3 of the single "Project Description" question rather than as separate sub-questions, plus one standalone field: **"Role of the LLM in Your Project"**. Required materials by track are listed in **"Track Materials ↔ How to Fill the Form"** in this chapter.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: I can't answer the target-user and validation parts — my entry is just a strategy / tool.</summary>

You still need to write them, but you do not need real users yet. Name a specific user segment, not "all traders". Usage / distribution results may be **targets** (e.g. "aiming for 50 users in month one") as long as you label them as targets. Incomplete answers will not invalidate a submission, but they affect how judges assess maturity.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: I didn't get Qwen credits — do I still fill in "Role of the LLM in Your Project"?</summary>

Yes, but only describe the models you actually used and what they do (coding, extraction, signal reasoning, agent decisions, etc.). **You can skip the Qwen part; it does not affect judging.**

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: Do S1 participants need to fill in anything extra?</summary>

Select "Are you an S1 participant/team" = Yes, and fill in **"Substantive New Additions"**; simple rename/resubmit is invalid. See **Q: Can I resubmit an S1 project with minor changes?** above.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: How do I enter the University Special Prize?</summary>

For university students / clubs / campus teams: submit to any main track and fill in the full university name in **"University Name"**. **If the entry already won a main-track prize, it is no longer eligible for University Special Prize**; leave university name blank and you won't be evaluated.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: How do I apply for Demo Day?</summary>

Check **"Apply for Demo Day"** in Google Form at submission; **all teams may check this box**. Post-event **priority invitation for winning or high-scoring quality projects**; final list selected by ops / judges. See **Chapter II · I. For Participants · Beyond Cash Prizes**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Q: Is there a Token participation prize?</summary>

Yes. It has two separate parts:

* **Qwen build credits during the competition:** Apply through the separate Google Form \[TBD: Qwen application link]. Bitget checks KYC status every 24 hours; the first 300 teams that apply and pass Bitget KYC may receive 30U-equivalent credits via the official Telegram community. Applying for Qwen is not hackathon registration and is not required to compete.
* **K3 post-event subsidy:** Select “Apply for K3 Token Subsidy” in the project submission form. Opted-in teams whose projects are confirmed as valid submissions may receive 30U-equivalent credits after the event.
* Teams qualifying for both may receive up to **60U equivalent in Token credits**.

</details>
